## 1. Purpose and method

This log records the AI design critique of the dashboard and how each suggestion was handled.

1. The initial dashboard (plain layout, default Plotly styling) was captured in `evidence/before/`. The critique below was written from those screenshots, which show the year and country filters, the four summary cards, the trend chart, the Top 10 and Bottom 10 bars, the scatter plots, the histogram, and the heatmap.
2. The initial dashboard was evaluated against four criteria:
   1. **Data-ink ratio:** is ink spent on data rather than decoration?
   2. **Colour accessibility:** is colour readable, distinguishable, and not the only carrier of meaning?
   3. **Layout hierarchy:** does the layout guide the eye from the most to the least important content?
   4. **Interactivity affordances:** is it obvious what can be done, and does the interface give feedback?
3. Each suggestion was reviewed critically, then **accepted** or **rejected** with a reason. Accepted suggestions were implemented and tested.
4. The improved dashboard was captured in `evidence/after/`.

### Honest scope note

- The suggestions were generated by the AI assistant and then reviewed by the author.
- The AI assistant ran its functional tests in a headless browser using **sample data with the same columns as `HDI_cleaned.csv`**, because it had no access to the real file. Entries marked "tested on sample data" must be re-confirmed on the real dataset (see the checklist in Section 8).
- Two changes (country trend comparison, and hiding the rankings and distribution when countries are selected) were requested by the author rather than raised by the critique. They are listed for completeness and marked as such.

## 3. Criterion 1: Data-ink ratio

| ID | Suggestion | Status | Reason | Implementation | Result after testing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| DI-1 | Print each value at the end of the Top 10 and Bottom 10 bars, and fix the axis to 0–1 | Accepted | Direct labels remove the need to estimate values from gridlines, and a fixed scale makes bars comparable between years | `texttemplate="%{x:.3f}"`, `textposition="outside"`, x-axis range 0–1.1 in `src/visualization.py` | Value labels render at the bar ends; tested on sample data |
| DI-2 | Merge the four separate bordered summary cards into one strip with hairline dividers | Accepted | Four boxed cards repeat border ink around four short numbers | `.kpi-strip` with single border and dividers in `style.css` | Strip renders as one block; tested on sample data |
| DI-3 | In comparison mode, move the legend below the chart | Accepted | A side legend takes plot width from the data | Horizontal legend placed under the axis in `create_hdi_trend` | Legend shows under the chart with the all-country average entry; tested on sample data |

## 4. Criterion 2: Colour accessibility

| ID | Suggestion | Status | Reason | Implementation | Result after testing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| CA-1 | Replace Plotly's default colours with a colour-blind-safe palette | Accepted | Several lines or points must stay distinguishable for colour-vision deficiencies | Okabe-Ito palette `PALETTE` applied to all Plotly charts | Palette applied; comparison lines are visibly distinct on sample data |
| CA-2 | Do not rely on colour alone to tell countries apart | Accepted | Colour-only encoding fails for some viewers | All-country average drawn as a dashed line; legend always shown; country names printed on scatter points when 12 or fewer are selected | Dashed line, legend, and point labels render; tested on sample data |
| CA-3 | Match the Seaborn histogram colour to the Plotly palette | Accepted | The histogram used a different default blue, which looked inconsistent | `color=PALETTE[0]` in `sns.histplot` | Chart colour matches the palette |
| CA-4 | Check text contrast, and darken muted grey text and the primary button | Accepted | Small grey text and white-on-blue buttons need adequate contrast | Muted text `#556270` (about 6:1 on white) and button `#0077C8` with white text (about 4.7:1), both calculated by hand | Meets the 4.5:1 guideline by calculation; not measured with a tool |
| CA-5 | Give keyboard users a clearly visible focus indicator | Accepted | The default focus ring was easy to miss | Dark ring with a white gap on buttons and filter controls in `style.css` | Not checked with a keyboard on the real dashboard; **to confirm** |
| CA-6 | Colour the Bottom 10 bars orange or red to signal "low" | Rejected | Colour would add a value judgement the bars and labels already convey, and would imply a warning state | Both ranking charts use the same blue | Not applicable |
| CA-7 | Replace the `coolwarm` heatmap palette with a sequential palette | Rejected | All correlations here are positive, so a diverging red-blue scale suggests a sign change that is not there. However, changing it would alter an existing required chart, and every cell is annotated with its value, so colour is not the only carrier of meaning | `coolwarm` kept as in the original script | Limitation recorded in Section 7 |

## 5. Criterion 3: Layout hierarchy

| ID | Suggestion | Status | Reason | Implementation | Result after testing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| LH-1 | Use a compact title band so the title is the clear top level | Accepted | The initial title was plain text with a rule beneath it | Navy header with title, subtitle, and project tag | Renders as designed; tested on sample data |
| LH-2 | Group the charts into labelled sections with one-line descriptions | Accepted | A flat grid of nine charts has no reading order | Sections: HDI over time, Rankings, Development indicators and HDI, Distribution and correlation | Sections render in order; tested on sample data |
| LH-3 | Make the summary figures larger and the labels smaller | Accepted | The key numbers should be read first | 30 px values with 13 px muted labels | Renders as designed |
| LH-4 | Use sentence-case filter labels instead of small capitals | Accepted | Capitals are harder to read and look like decoration | "Year" and "Countries to compare", 13 px, semibold | Renders as designed |
| LH-5 | Centre the heatmap and limit its width when it is the only chart in its section | Accepted | A full-width heatmap becomes oversized | `.chart-card.centered` with a maximum width of 820 px | Heatmap centred in comparison mode; tested on sample data |
| LH-6 | Increase the font size of the Seaborn charts to match the Plotly charts | Deferred | Seaborn text is small compared with the Plotly text. A fix means changing the figure code (for example `sns.set_context`) | Not implemented | Listed as future work |

**Testing note (layout bug).** While testing, the chart areas collapsed to zero height after a filter was applied. Dash puts an inline `height: 100%` on graphs, which overrode the intended height inside the loading wrapper. Fixed by forcing heights of 400 px (440 px for the trend) in `style.css`. After the fix, the chart heights stayed constant across Apply and Reset in the headless test on sample data.

## 6. Criterion 4: Interactivity affordances

| ID | Suggestion | Status | Reason | Implementation | Result after testing |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| IA-1 | Show a hint when a filter has changed but has not been applied | Accepted | Apply/Reset gives no signal that the charts are out of date | Amber "You changed a filter. Press Apply" message, driven by a callback that compares current and applied filters | Hint appeared after a filter change and disappeared after Apply; tested on sample data |
| IA-2 | Show a status label for the current view | Accepted | Users should know whether they are seeing all countries or a selection | "Global view: all 195 countries, 2023" or "Comparing N countries" | Label updated correctly after Apply and Reset |
| IA-3 | Explain under the filter bar what each filter affects | Accepted | The country filter does not affect every chart, which is confusing if unstated | Dynamic note under the filter bar | Note changes between the global and comparison views |
| IA-4 | Add loading indicators | Accepted | The Seaborn images take a moment to redraw | `dcc.Loading` around every chart | Spinners wrap each chart; tested on sample data |
| IA-5 | Keep the filter bar visible while scrolling | Accepted | The filters should stay reachable on a long page | Sticky filter bar on screens wider than 900 px | Not checked while scrolling on the real dashboard; **to confirm** |
| IA-6 | Label scatter points by country when only a few are selected | Accepted | With two or three countries, hover-only identification is slow | Text labels on points when 12 or fewer countries are selected | Labels appear for the selected countries; tested on sample data |
| IA-7 | Make the summary figures follow the selected scope, and show change since 1990 | Accepted | Whole-dataset figures are misleading beside a country selection | KPI labels and values switch to "(selected)"; the average card shows the change since 1990 | Labels and values switched correctly in comparison mode |
| IA-8 | Show each selected country's HDI trend against the all-country average *(author request)* | Accepted | A single global average line does not answer country-level questions | One line per country plus a dashed average in `create_hdi_trend` | Three selected countries gave three lines plus the dashed average; tested on sample data |
| IA-9 | Hide the Top 10, Bottom 10, and HDI Distribution charts while countries are selected *(author request)* | Accepted | These charts describe all 195 countries, so showing them beside a country selection is misleading. The author gave explicit permission to hide them, an exception to the original "keep all nine charts" rule. All nine reappear on Reset | Rankings section removed from the page and rebuilt on Reset; the distribution image is hidden | Hidden after Apply with countries and shown at the correct size after Reset; tested on sample data |
| IA-10 | Remove the Apply button and update the charts as soon as a filter changes | Rejected | The brief specifies Apply and Reset, and instant updates would regenerate the Seaborn images on every selection | Apply and Reset kept, with the pending hint (IA-1) instead | Not applicable |
| IA-11 | Click a bar or point to add that country to the selection (cross-filtering) | Rejected | Valuable, but beyond the scope of this project and risky to build late | Not implemented | Listed as future work |

**Testing note (hidden-chart bug).** When the rankings were first hidden with CSS, they came back at zero height after Reset, because Plotly cannot size a chart drawn inside a hidden container. The fix was to remove the section from the page when countries are selected and rebuild it on Reset. After the fix, the charts returned at their normal size in the headless test on sample data.

## 7. Known limitations after the critique

- The `coolwarm` heatmap palette (CA-7) can make uniformly positive correlations look like they change sign.
- The Seaborn charts have smaller text than the Plotly charts (LH-6).
- Some missing values in the source data were interpolated during preprocessing, and they are not flagged in the cleaned dataset.
- Accessibility was assessed by calculation and by inspection. No automated accessibility checker or screen-reader test was run.
